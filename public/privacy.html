Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 27, 2026 • Version 1.0
SUMMARY: MoodFlix operates with minimal data footprint. We do not sell your personal data or store passwords. Authentication is handled securely through Google OAuth via Supabase.

1. Information We Collect

When you access or use MoodFlix, we may collect the following types of information:

  • Account Data: When you authenticate via Google Sign-In, we receive basic profile info (email address and public profile identifier) through Supabase Authentication. We never receive or store your Google account password.
  • Client-Side Preferences & Diagnostic State: Selected movie moods, diagnostic answers, search filters, and playback configurations are processed and saved locally in your browser (LocalStorage / IndexedDB / OPFS).
  • Transfer & Library Data: Information regarding saved torrent metadata or watch history is stored in your personal Supabase account library or local browser cache for synchronization across your sessions.

2. How We Use Information

We use the minimal collected information solely for:

  • Authenticating your session and securing account access.
  • Generating personalized movie and television recommendations based on your diagnostic answers.
  • Maintaining your saved library, watchlist, and transfer records across your devices.

3. Third-Party Services

MoodFlix integrates with trusted third-party providers:

  • Google OAuth: Secure authentication provider for single sign-on.
  • Supabase: Cloud database and authentication infrastructure.
  • The Movie Database (TMDB): Public media metadata provider for posters, ratings, overviews, and release schedules.

4. Data Storage and Security

We implement industry-standard encryption protocols (HTTPS / TLS) for all network communications. Authentication tokens are handled via secure JWTs managed by Supabase. We do not host, store, or distribute copyrighted video files on our servers.

5. Your Rights and Data Deletion

You may request the deletion of your account and synchronized data at any time by signing out and clearing your browser storage, or by submitting a data removal request to our team.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions or inquiries regarding this Privacy Policy, you can reach out via our repository or contact us at support@moodflix.app.
